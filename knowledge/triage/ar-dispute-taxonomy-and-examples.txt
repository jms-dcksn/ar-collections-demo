AR dispute taxonomy and examples

Classification rules

Return po_mismatch only when invoice and purchase-order price, quantity, tax, or authorized amount conflict.
Return missing_pod only when payment is blocked because delivery evidence is requested or missing.
Return payment_misapplication only when the customer reports payment but the target invoice remains open because application is absent or incorrect.
Return unsupported when the evidence does not establish one supported category.
Return unsupported and request manual triage when confidence is below 0.75.
Never infer payment-system application details from a customer's statement alone.

po_mismatch

Definition
A purchase-order mismatch is a specific conflict between an invoice and the governing purchase order in price, quantity, tax, or authorized amount.

Positive signals
The invoice and purchase order are both identified, and their comparable values show a specific difference. The customer may cite an unauthorized charge, excess billed quantity, wrong unit price, tax difference, or invoice total above the authorized amount.

Exclusions
Do not use po_mismatch for a general claim that a balance looks wrong without purchase-order evidence. Do not use it when the only blocker is missing delivery evidence or when a reported payment was applied incorrectly.

Example 1
AR-PO-001: Northstar Manufacturing disputes INV-10471 because the invoice amount is $48,750 while the purchase order authorizes $47,250. The documented $1,500 conflict supports po_mismatch.

Example 2
Fictional neutral pattern: An invoice bills 120 units while its purchase order authorizes 100 units at the same unit price. The documented quantity conflict supports po_mismatch.

missing_pod

Definition
A missing proof-of-delivery dispute exists when payment is blocked because the customer requests delivery evidence or the expected proof of delivery is absent from the case packet.

Positive signals
The customer explicitly requests proof of delivery, denies having delivery documentation, or states that payment will remain on hold until delivery date, recipient, signature, shipment, or quantity evidence is supplied.

Exclusions
Do not use missing_pod for a price or quantity conflict against a purchase order. Do not use it merely because a shipment is mentioned when the actual blocker is a reported payment that remains unapplied.

Example 1
AR-POD-002: Riverbend Retail is holding INV-20482 pending delivery evidence. The synthetic record shows delivery on June 18, 2026, signed by M. Chen, with shipment and invoice quantities matching. The request for that evidence supports missing_pod.

Example 2
Fictional neutral pattern: A customer says an invoice can be released only after receiving the carrier delivery date and recipient signature. The explicit delivery-evidence request supports missing_pod.

payment_misapplication

Definition
A payment misapplication dispute exists when the customer reports a payment, the intended target invoice remains open, and payment-system evidence establishes that application is absent or incorrect.

Positive signals
The case identifies a payment reference and target invoice, while trusted cash-application evidence shows the payment is unapplied or applied to a different invoice. A matching payment amount and remittance strengthen the classification.

Exclusions
Do not infer payment-system application details from the customer's statement alone. Do not use payment_misapplication when no payment is reported, the target invoice is not identified, or the only supported conflict is with a purchase order or delivery evidence.

Example 1
AR-PAY-003: Summit Medical Distribution reports a $36,800 payment under PAY-77821 for target invoice INV-30915. The customer report alone is insufficient; payment-system evidence that the payment was applied to another invoice supports payment_misapplication.

Example 2
Fictional neutral pattern: A customer supplies payment reference PAY-54190 and remittance for INV-55230. A trusted lookup shows the matching payment on INV-55211 while INV-55230 remains open. The incorrect application supports payment_misapplication.

Ambiguous and unsupported guidance

AR-AMB-004 says only that the balance does not look right and asks for investigation. It contains no discriminating purchase-order, delivery-evidence, or payment-application facts, so return unsupported and request manual triage. Apply the same result whenever evidence does not establish exactly one supported category or confidence is below 0.75.
